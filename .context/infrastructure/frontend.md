### Test-ID strategy — **exists, `data-testid` convention confirmed**

Grepped `data-testid` across `components/` — matched in 20+ files (search capped at 20 results by tooling; the true count is at least that many). Confirmed genuine usage, not a false positive from an unrelated string. Sample evidence from `components/settings/TokensList.tsx`:

```tsx
<Card data-testid="tokens-list" role="region" aria-labelledby="settings-tokens-heading">
  ...
  data-testid="issue-token-open"
  ...
<CardContent className="..." data-testid="tokens-error">
  ...
  data-testid="tokens-retry"
  ...
<CardContent className="..." data-testid="tokens-empty">
  ...
  data-testid="tokens-empty-issue"
```

**Convention**: kebab-case, semantic/purpose-based naming (`tokens-list`, `tokens-error`, `tokens-retry`, `tokens-empty`, `issue-token-open`) rather than component-type-based. Also frequently paired with ARIA roles (`role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) on the same element — a genuinely accessibility-conscious pattern, not just a testing hook bolted on. This is directly usable by `/adapt-framework` as the selector-contract convention for KATA `Page` components: prefer `[data-testid="<name>"]` locators over CSS/text selectors wherever a `data-testid` exists.

Files confirmed with the convention (partial list, capped at 20 by the search tool — more likely exist): `components/ui/meter.tsx`, `components/traceability/TraceabilityChainView.tsx`, `components/tests/TestDetailView.tsx`, `components/tests/TestReorderClient.tsx`, `components/tests/TestTagEditor.tsx`, `components/tests/NewTestBuilder.tsx`, `components/tests/StartRunButton.tsx`, `components/tests/TestDetailTabs.tsx`, `components/settings/WorkspacesList.tsx`, `components/tests/AtcChainPicker.tsx`, `components/tests/ChainedAtcCard.tsx`, `components/settings/RevokeTokenModal.tsx`, `components/settings/SettingsNav.tsx`, `components/settings/TokensList.tsx`, `components/settings/IssueTokenModal.tsx`, `components/settings/LeaveWorkspaceModal.tsx`, `components/settings/NotificationPreferencesGrid.tsx`, `components/settings/ComingSoon.tsx`, `components/settings/IdentityCard.tsx`, `components/runs/RunHistoryView.tsx`.
